# Brand — Stonk Ledger

The receipt and the reinvestment for meme coins that pay dividends in tokenized stocks.

_Generated by `brand-design` on 2026-09-14 after a `design-taste` direction pass. Update by re-running the skills._

## Direction (from design-taste)

```
Direction: paper statement. Light, warm off-white ground, near-black ink,
           one accent (oxblood). Gradient Trust warmth with Stark Minimal restraint.
           Neobrutalist touches confined to the receipt and the approval box.
Density:   comfortable on pages, compact inside the payout table
Surface:   flat sections separated by hairline rules, like a printed ledger.
           Bordered panels only for the DRIP approval block and the share card.
Type mood: ledger, tabular, editorial. Sans for labels, mono numerals everywhere,
           serif display for the one big number per page.
Motion:    crisp and functional, under 200ms, no bounce.
Do:        one big number above the fold; a proof link at the end of every counted row;
           2px ink border + 4px hard offset shadow on the approve button and the share
           card only; 4px corners; ruled rows; right-aligned tabular numbers;
           "estimated" and "probable" as small plain labels, never colors.
Don't:     StonkFun green, Solana purple, gradients, glows, blur; green/red for payouts
           (they are income, not PnL); card grids for the statement; chart decoration;
           emoji; the words yield or APR anywhere.
```

Why not a StonkFun clone: StonkFun is a dark launchpad grid. Stonk Ledger is a document plus one action. A statement that looks like a statement stands out among dark crypto dashboards and matches the trust job.

## Typography — Instrument Serif + Inter + JetBrains Mono

- **Display (the one big number and page titles):** Instrument Serif, weight 400, via `font-serif`
- **Interface and body:** Inter, via `font-sans`
- **Numbers, addresses, signatures:** JetBrains Mono, via `font-mono tabular-nums`

Wired through `next/font/google` in `app/layout.tsx`. CSS variables `--font-sans`, `--font-mono`, `--font-serif`; Tailwind theme also maps `--font-heading` to the serif.

## Gradients (not used)

The mood is serious and the surface is paper. Gradients would fight the intent. None were generated.

## Neobrutalist accents

Exactly two objects carry a 2px `border-foreground` border and a hard offset shadow (`shadow-[4px_4px_0_var(--foreground)]`, 3px on the button): the Stock DRIP approval panel with its approve button, and the OG share card. Nothing else. The rest of the app is flat sections with `border-b border-border` hairlines.

## Tone and voice

### Words to use

Direct, specific, number-forward, factual. Prefer verbs over adjectives and nouns over metaphors. Say what a thing does. "Payouts", "dividends received", "stock received", "proof", "approve", "revoke", "cap", "fee".

### Words to avoid

Yield, APR, APY, earn, returns, guaranteed, unlock, supercharge, moon, degen. No emoji. No exclamation marks. Never describe payouts as income the holder is owed; they depend on trading volume, and the UI says so.

### Voice example

> TREE has paid this wallet 0.8312 APPLx since Sep 6. 146 payouts, each with a proof link. Stock DRIP is on: STONK to SPYx, cap 1,000 STONK, 1% fee, revoke any time.

## Usage dos and don'ts

**Do:**

- Use the shadcn CSS variables (`bg-background`, `text-foreground`, `bg-primary`) everywhere. Never hardcode hex.
- Keep the 4px spacing grid (`gap-2`, `p-4`, `px-6`).
- Use `font-mono tabular-nums` for any number, right-aligned in tables.
- Follow the `number-formatting` skill: shares to four decimals, USD to two.
- Put a Solscan link on every counted transaction and every DRIP run signature.

**Don't:**

- Hardcode colors in component files.
- Use `transition-all`; name the property.
- Add a dark-mode toggle before the brand pass on dark surfaces is done. The app is light; `.dark` exists for the share card.
- Use green or red for payout amounts.
- Use gradients, glows, or blur anywhere.
